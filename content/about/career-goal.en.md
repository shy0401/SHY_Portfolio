## Overview

I am preparing for the Police Officer Candidate Exam while building a path toward a cyber investigation role. My goal is not only to handle technical tools, but also to translate analysis results into documents and explanations that support legal and administrative judgment.

In cyber investigation, I believe it is important to reconstruct incident flow, preserve evidence integrity, and explain findings clearly to non-specialists. For that reason, my learning combines technical practice with structured documentation.

| Stage | Preparation | Evidence of Progress |
