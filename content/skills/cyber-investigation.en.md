Cyber investigation preparation connects web hacking, reversing, and forensics into a single ability: understanding and explaining an incident. While preparing for the Korean police patrol officer exam, I aim to build judgment, responsibility, physical readiness, interview communication, and documentation skills required in public security.

I have completed the English and Korean history proof requirements and am now focusing on the first-stage written subjects: criminal law, constitutional law, and police studies. I also manage physical routines and interview-answer keywords for the later stages.

## Analysis Report Practice Flow

| Step | Question | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Collection | What logs, files, screens, or proof items were collected? | Evidence list and original path |
| 2. Integrity | Are original and working copies separated with hashes recorded? | Hash value, collection time, storage location |
| 3. Analysis | What evidence confirms vulnerability, behavior, or traces? | Analysis table, screenshots, reproduction conditions |
| 4. Timeline | In what order did the actions occur? | Time-ordered incident flow |
| 5. Judgment | Are facts and assumptions separated? | Reasoning and limitations |
| 6. Reporting | Can non-specialists understand the finding? | Summary, response proposal, follow-up checks |
