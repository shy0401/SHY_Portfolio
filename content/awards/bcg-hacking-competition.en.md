## Overview

This page records my participation in the 2021 IT Information Engineering BCG Hacking Competition during my first year. The competition was held online, and participants solved security challenges within a limited time to earn points. I received the silver prize, ranking 2nd by points.

This experience became an early starting point for my web hacking, reversing, and forensics learning. It taught me to read hints carefully, compare possible approaches, and leave evidence-based writeups instead of only focusing on the final answer.

## Meaning in My Current Roadmap

The BCG competition is one of my earliest security learning milestones. At the time, I focused on solving the problems. Now I reinterpret the same experience through the structure `situation → clue → hypothesis → reproduction → evidence → response perspective`.

This record is organized as early evidence that connects to my later KISA/Honam cybersecurity achievements and cyber investigation roadmap.
